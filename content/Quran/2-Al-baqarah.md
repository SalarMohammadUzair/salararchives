> 3. Human Offspring vs. Satan’s Offspring
Many scholars (such as Ibn Kathir and Al-Tabari) explain that the plural command "Get down (ihbitu)"—which is used for three or more people—includes the progeny of both sides. It means your children and his (Satan's) followers will be in a state of conflict.
 >> The "foe" part is essentially a **divine warning**. Allah is telling us:

>>1. **Don't be surprised by hardship:** Earth is not a place of pure peace like Paradise; it is a place of struggle.
    
>>2. **Know your real enemy:** While humans may fight each other, the ultimate "enemy" who wants to see everyone fail is Satan.
    
>>3. **It is temporary:** The verse ends by saying this state is only "for a time" (until death or the Day of Judgment).
