Allah opens his light to the self aware
In the ending of Fatihah, we ask Allah
Oh Allah guide us, and in response  at the start of this surah, Allah shows the path, this book, this is the one that contains the guide. 
## verse 1
> This is the Book; in it is guidance sure, without doubt, to those who fear Allah;
### Reflection 
Right from the get go, Allah promotes self awareness as I take it to be. The one who fears Allah is the one that is self aware enough to realise the state of being he is in and how he interacts with Allah.
