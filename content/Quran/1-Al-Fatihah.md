The ultimate prayer. 
Oh my Lord, please do not put me on the path of the devil, do not ever abandon me so thank maybe end up in the vices of the westerner. 
Oh Allah,  please bestow upon me the khilafat,  entrust it to me and please allow me to be able to carry out your command. 

---


## Verse 1
> In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.

## Verse 2
> Praise be to Allah, the Cherisher and Sustainer of the worlds;

## Verse 3
> Most Gracious, Most Merciful;

## Verse 4
> Master of the Day of Judgment.

## Verse 5
> Thee do we worship, and Thine aid we seek.

## Verse 6
> Show us the straight way,

## Verse 7
> The way of those on whom Thou hast bestowed Thy Grace, those whose (portion) is not wrath, and who go not astray.
