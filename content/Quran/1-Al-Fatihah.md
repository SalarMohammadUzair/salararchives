The ultimate prayer. 
Oh my Lord, please do not put me on the path of the devil, do not ever abandon me so thank maybe end up in the vices of the westerner. 
Oh Allah,  please bestow upon me the khilafat,  entrust it to me and please allow me to be able to carry out your command.
