<div class="poem-container" style="text-align: center; font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.8; margin: 2em 0;">
  
  <h2 style="margin-bottom: 0.2em;">KHirad-mandon se kya puchhun ki meri ibtida kya hai</h2>
  <p style="color: gray; font-style: italic; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 2em;">Allama Iqbal</p>

  <p>
    KHirad-mandon se kya puchhun ki meri ibtida kya hai<br>
    ki main is fikr mein rahta hun meri intiha kya hai
  </p>

  <p>
    KHudi ko kar buland itna ki har taqdir se pahle<br>
    KHuda bande se KHud puchhe bata teri raza kya hai
  </p>

  <p>
    maqam-e-guftugu kya hai agar main kimiya-gar hun<br>
    yahi soz-e-nafas hai aur meri kimiya kya hai
  </p>

  <p>
    nazar aain mujhe taqdir ki gahraiyan is mein<br>
    na puchh ai ham-nashin mujh se wo chashm-e-surma-sa kya hai
  </p>

  <p>
    agar hota wo majzub-e-farangi is zamane mein<br>
    to 'iqbaal' us ko samjhata maqam-e-kibriya kya hai
  </p>

  <p>
    nawa-e-subh-gahi ne jigar KHun kar diya mera<br>
    KHudaya jis KHata ki ye saza hai wo KHata kya hai
  </p>

</div>
