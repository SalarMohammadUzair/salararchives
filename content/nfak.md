**The Chisti Order** (the specific sufi lineage that gave birth to qawalli) was unusal in the islamic world because they took music seriously as a path to God when most other strands of Islamic thought were suspicious of it. They justified it through a specific theology: that the human soul came from God and carries a memory of that origin, and that music can awaken that memory.
