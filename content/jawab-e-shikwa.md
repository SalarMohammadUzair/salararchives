  <h3>STANZA 1 - Jawab-e-Shikwa by Allama Iqbal</h3>
  <p dir="rtl" lang="ur" style="font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.8;">
    دل سے جو بات نکلتی ہے اثر رکھتی ہے<br>
    پر نہیں طاقت پرواز مگر رکھتی ہے<br>
    قدسی الاصل ہے رفعت پہ نظر رکھتی ہے<br>
    خاک سے اٹھتی ہے گردوں پہ گزر رکھتی ہے<br>
    عشق تھا فتنہ گر و سرکش و چالاک مرا<br>
    آسماں چیر گیا نالۂ بیباک مرا
  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">
    [Dil se jo baat nikalti hai asar rakhti hai]<br>
    [Par nahi taqat-e-parwaaz magar rakhti hai]<br>
    [Qudsi al-asl hai rufat pe nazar rakhti hai]<br>
    [Khaak se uthti hai gardoon pe guzar rakhti hai]<br>
    [Ishq tha fitna gar-o-sarkash-o-chaalak mera]<br>
    [Aasman cheer gaya nala-e-bebaak mera]
  </p>
  <p>
    Words spoken from the heart always have an effect<br>
    They don’t have wings and yet have the power to fly<br>
    Sacred and pure their origin, on lofty heights their sights are set<br>
    They rise from dust and pierce through the sky<br>
    My love was insolent, mischievous and sly,<br>
    Yet my outcry rose and pierced the sky
  </p>

  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">
    [Peer-e-gardoon ne kaha sun ke kaheen hai koi]<br>
    [Bole sayyare sar-e-arsh bareen hai koi]<br>
    [Chaand kehta tha nahi ahl-e-zameen hai koi]<br>
    [Kehkashan kehti thi posheeda yehin hai koi]<br>
    [Kuch jo samjha mere shikwe ko to rizwan samjha]<br>
    [Mujh ko jannat se nikaala hua insaan samjha]
  </p>
  <p>
    Listening to the outcry, the ancient heaven said, “There is someone somewhere”<br>
    The planets spoke, “There is someone on top of the exalted throne”<br>
    The moon said, “No, it is someone from the Earth below”<br>
    The galaxy said, “It must be someone hidden here we do not know”<br>
    Only the gatekeeper of paradise recongnized some of my plaint<br>
    And understood that I was the man thrown out of paradise
  </p>

  <h3>STANZA 3 - Jawab-e-Shikwa by Allama Iqbal</h3>

    تھی فرشتوں کو بھی حیرت کہ یہ آواز ہے کیا<br>
    عرش والوں پہ بھی کھلتا نہیں یہ راز ہے کیا<br>

    آ گئی خاک کی چٹکی کو بھی پرواز ہے کیا<br>

  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">

    [Arsh walon pe bhi khulta nahi yeh raaz hai kya]<br>
    [Ta sar-e-arsh bhi insaan ki tag-o-taaz hai kya]<br>
    [Aa gayi khaak ki chhatki ko bhi parwaaz hai kya]<br>

    [Shoq-o-gustaakh yeh pasti ke makeen kaise hain]
  </p>
  <p>
    Even the angels wondered what this voice was<br>
    Nor other dwellers of the sky could unravel this secret<br>
    [They wondered] Have celestial heights become the aim of a man’s striving?<br>
    Have this pinch of dust learnt how to fly?<br>
    These earth dwellers, how little of manners do they know!<br>
    How cheeky and insolent are these habitants of regions down below
  </p>

  <h3>STANZA 4 - Jawab-e-Shikwa by Allama Iqbal</h3>
  <p dir="rtl" lang="ur" style="font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.8;">
    اس قدر شوخ کہ اللہ سے بھی برہم ہے<br>

    عالم کیف ہے دانائے رموز کم ہے<br>
    ہاں مگر عجز کے اسرار سے نامحرم ہے<br>
    ناز ہے طاقت گفتار پہ انسانوں کو<br>
    بات کرنے کا سلیقہ نہیں نادانوں کو
  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">
    [Is qadar shokh ke Allah se bhi barham hai]<br>
    [Tha jo masjood-e-malaik yeh wohi aadam hai]<br>
    [Aalam-e-kaif hai dana-e-ramooz kam hai]<br>
    [Haan magar ajz ke asraar se na-mahram hai]<br>
    [Naz hai taqat-e-guftaar pe insaano ko]<br>
    [Baat karne ka saleeqa nahi nadano ko]
  </p>
  <p>

    It he the same Adam before whom the angels bowed?<br>
    He knows about things, their quantity and quality<br>
    Yes, these he knows; but nothing of the secret of humility<br>
    Humans are proud of their power of speech<br>
    But of the manner of speaking, they are quite ignorant
  </p>

  <h3>STANZA 5 - Jawab-e-Shikwa by Allama Iqbal</h3>

  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">

    Your tears tremble at the brim and are ready to flow<br>
    Your cry has encompassed the heavens<br>
    How cunning is the tongue of your impassioned heart<br>
    You expressed your complaint with great eloquence<br>
    You raised man to celestial heights to converse and speak directly with God
  </p>

    [Tarbiat aam tou hai johar-e-qaabil hi nahi]<br>
    [Jis se taameer ho aadam ki yeh woh gul hi nahi]<br>

  <p>

    Training is widespread, but there is no talent to refine<br>
    This is not the clay that created Adam<br>
    If someone was worthy, we would raise him to regal splendour<br>
    Those who seek, We would unveil a new world of wonder
  </p>

  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">
    [Kis qadar tum pe giran subah ki bedari hai]<br>
    [Hum se kab pyaar hai haan neend tumhein pyari hai]<br>
    [Taba-e-azad pe qaid-e-Ramazan bhaari hai]<br>
    [Tumhein keh do yehi aaeen-e-wafa-dari hai]<br>

  </p>
  <p>
    How burdensome is the dawn’s awakening upon you, how loth you are to rise?<br>
    When do you love us? It is your slumber that you prize<br>
    On your uncontrolled spirit, Ramadan’s fast does heavily press<br>
    Can you really say this is the way of faithfulness<br>
    A people are bound by faith; without faith they cease to be<br>
    If nothing binds you, you will be like meteors, not stars in a galaxy
  </p>

    If as traders of tombstones you have earned such renown<br>
    What is there to stop you the trading of idols made of stone?
  </p>

    There is none amongst you who truly desires heavenly maidens<br>
    [It is as if] The radiance of Mount Sinai exists, but there is no Moses to behold it

  <p dir="rtl" lang="ur" style="font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.8;">
    منفعت ایک ہے اس قوم کا نقصان بھی ایک<br>

    حرم پاک بھی اللہ بھی قرآن بھی ایک<br>
    کچھ بڑی بات تھی ہوتے جو مسلمان بھی ایک<br>

  </p>
  <p style="color: #666; font-style: italic;">
    [Manfa’at ek hai is qaum ka nuksaan bhi ek]<br>
    [Ek hi sab ka nabi deen bhi imaan bhi ek]<br>
    [Haram-e-pak bhi Allah bhi Quran bhi ek]<br>
    [Kuch barri baat thi hote jo musalman bhi ek]<br>
    [Firqabandi hai kahin aur kahin zaatain hain]<br>
    [Kya zamane mein panapne ki yahi baatain hain]
  </p>
  <p>
    You are one people, you share your weal and woes<br>
