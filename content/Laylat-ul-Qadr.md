## The Quran

> [!quote] Surah Al-Qadr (97)
>
> - 97:1 — Indeed, We sent down the Quran on the Night of Decree.
> - 97:2 — And what will make you know what the Night of Decree is?
> - 97:3 — The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.
> - 97:4 — The angels and the Spirit (Jibreel) descend therein, by permission of their Lord, for every matter.
> - 97:5 — It is peace until the rising of dawn.

## Traditions

> [!quote] Bukhari 35 — Virtue & Forgiveness
> Narrated by Abu Hurayrah: Whoever stood in prayer on the Night of Qadr out of sincere faith and hoping for reward from Allah, then all his previous sins will be forgiven.

> [!quote] Bukhari 2017 — Seek in Odd Nights
> Narrated by Aisha: The Prophet said, "Search for the Night of Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten days of Ramadan."

> [!quote] Bukhari 2015 — Last Seven Nights
> Narrated by Ibn Umar: Some men among the companions were shown in their dreams that the Night of Qadr was in the last seven nights of Ramadan. The Prophet said, "It seems that all your dreams agree that it is in the last seven nights, so whoever wants to search for it should search in the last seven nights of Ramadan."

> [!quote] Bukhari 1917–1918 — Nine, Seven, Five Days Left
> Narrated by Ibn Abbas: The Prophet said, "Seek it in the last ten nights of Ramadan, when there are nine days left, and seven days left, and five days left."

> [!quote] Bukhari 2016 / 2027 — The Mud and Water Sign
> Narrated by Abu Salama from Abu Said: The Prophet said, "I was informed of the date of the Night of Qadr but I was caused to forget it. So look for it in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan." Then a cloud came and it rained, and the Prophet prostrated in mud and water, and his forehead bore a trace of mud.

> [!quote] Bukhari 1919 — The Quarrel That Withdrew the Knowledge
> Narrated by Ubadah ibn al-Samit: The Prophet said, "I came out to inform you about the Night of Qadr, but so-and-so and so-and-so were quarrelling, so the knowledge of it was taken away from me. Perhaps this is better for you. So seek it on the ninth and the seventh and the fifth."

> [!quote] Bukhari 1920 — Prophet's Intensive Worship
> Narrated by Aisha: When the last ten days of Ramadan came, the Prophet would stay up at night, wake his family, and gird his loins.

> [!quote] Muslim 760 — Virtue & Forgiveness
> Narrated by Abu Hurayrah: Whoever stands in prayer on the Night of Qadr with faith and seeking reward, his previous sins are forgiven.

> [!quote] Muslim 1167 / 1165 — Seek in Odd Nights
> Narrated by Aisha and Ibn Umar: Seek the Night of Qadr in the last ten nights, on the odd-numbered nights.

> [!quote] Muslim 1169 — The 23rd Night
> Narrated by Abdullah ibn Unais: The Prophet said, "I was shown Laylat al-Qadr then I was made to forget it." There was a downpour on the twenty-third night, and as the Prophet went back after leading prayer, there was a trace of water and clay on his forehead and nose.

> [!quote] Muslim 1174 — Prophet's Worship in Last Ten Nights
> Narrated by Aisha: The Prophet strove hard and girded his loins during the last ten nights, staying up and waking his family.
