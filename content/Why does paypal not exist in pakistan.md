The State Bank of Pakistan has some very rigid policies that it does not budge on.
it has made it hard for ALOT of companies to open operations in pakistan.
Not necessarily ill-will of the state bank, these policies are born out of necessity.

For instance, for the us, the federal reserve don't care about  how many moves, money moves in and out freely.
but if that were to happen in pakistan, ( capital flight, people moving dollars out fast), the economy would collapse.
That is why, they have policies that watch any forex movement closely, to make sure nothing happens against regulatory frameworks
Stripe and Paypal just decided that it is not worth the hassle to go through and spend money into building all that compliance machinery.
